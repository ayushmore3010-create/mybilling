Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At MyBillingSystem, we take your shop data privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, store, and safeguard your account details, product inventory, and customer invoices.

1. Data Collection

We collect information you explicitly provide when creating your account, including shop name, owner contact details, address, and GSTIN.

2. Data Security

All database records are isolated per shop using strict multi-tenant architecture. Passwords are protected using PBKDF2/Bcrypt cryptographic hashing.
