Under Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) regulations, every registered retailer and shop owner must issue a clear Tax Invoice for taxable sales containing mandatory details like GSTIN, invoice serial number, line item tax breakdown, and HSN/SAC codes.

Understanding CGST, SGST, and IGST

For sales made within the same state (Intra-state sales), GST is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For example, on an 18% GST product, 9% is CGST and 9% is SGST. For inter-state sales, full 18% is applied as IGST (Integrated GST).
