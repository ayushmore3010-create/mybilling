Effective inventory management is the secret to retail profitability. Running out of fast-selling stock leads to lost customers, while over-stocking slow items locks up cash flow.

5 Essential Stock Tips

  1. Set up automatic low-stock warnings (e.g. alert when quantity drops below 5 units).
  2. Categorize products logically (e.g. Groceries vs Dairy vs Household).
  3. Track purchase price vs selling price to maintain clear profit margins.
  4. Perform monthly stock audits using MyBillingSystem product inventory lists.
  5. Analyze top-selling products using sales analytics reports.
