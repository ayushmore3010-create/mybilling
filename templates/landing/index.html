Frequently Asked Questions

What is MyBillingSystem? ↓

MyBillingSystem is a simple and smart online SaaS billing platform designed specifically for Indian shop owners, Kirana stores, clothing outlets, mobile repair shops, and local retailers to quickly generate bills, track sales, and send WhatsApp invoices.

Can I create GST compliant invoices? ↓

Yes! You can configure GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and the system automatically calculates CGST, SGST, IGST, and taxable amounts for every bill.

Can I use MyBillingSystem on both mobile and laptop? ↓

Yes! All your data is saved in secure cloud database storage. Create a bill on your mobile phone, and it automatically syncs with your laptop in real-time.

How does WhatsApp invoice sharing work? ↓

After generating a bill, click "Send on WhatsApp". It generates a pre-formatted message with invoice details and a secure URL for your customer to open and download their bill directly.
